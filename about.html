Our story

Built on warmth, craft, and flavor.

We believe every meal should leave a lasting memory and every guest should feel right at home from the first bite to the final goodbye.

Since 2014

A neighborhood restaurant with a big heart.

Harvest & Hearth began with a simple idea: create a welcoming dining space where thoughtful food, genuine hospitality, and the joy of shared meals lead the experience.

Over the years, we’ve grown into a place where local ingredients, modern cooking, and old-school warmth come together beautifully. We serve soulful meals, handcrafted cocktails, and a relaxed atmosphere that makes every guest feel like part of the family.
